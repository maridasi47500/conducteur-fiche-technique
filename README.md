1. Liberation Through Automation

    AI generates random conductors and technical sheets (the rails db:seed)
    This frees human artists from bureaucratic repetition
    The real work — creating meaning, emotional resonance, interpretation — remains irreducibly human
    Artistic payoff: Demonstrates that efficiency tools don't diminish artistry; they expose it

2. Emotion as Irreducible Complexity

Your README asks: "Do you need to tell a story to convey emotions in music?"

    AI can catalog emotional responses (nuance notes via Python)
    AI can suggest patterns (search: Rock, tempo, dynamics)
    But AI cannot feel or truly create feeling — only humans can intuit what makes an audience's heart move
    The artwork: The gap between what AI suggests and what humans choose becomes visible

3. The Conductor as Intermediary

Conductors are translators between notation and human experience:

    AI: Manages the technical sheet, metadata, logistics
    Humans: Interpret the score through body, emotion, lived experience
    The artistic statement: Technology handles the mechanical; humans handle the meaningful

4. Meaning-Making as Resistance

In the digital age, meaning-making is scarce and valuable:

    AI automates the what (data, structure, suggestions)
    Humans own the why (intention, ethics, resonance)
    Your project could show: intelligent systems that know their place
